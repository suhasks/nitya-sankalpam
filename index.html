About

Nitya Sankalpam is an independent app based on the Mysuru/Karnataka Chandra-mana and Amavasyanta tradition. The 2026–27 reference period is 19 March 2026 through 6 April 2027. The app is not affiliated with or an official publication of Ontikoppal Panchanga Mandira.

Annual published Panchanga data is the validation authority. The calculation engine is separated from validated annual data so future editions can be added without changing the app UI.
