About

This is an independent project inspired by the Karnataka/Mysuru tradition and Ontikoppal Panchanga references. It is not affiliated with Ontikoppal Panchanga Mandira. Annual published Panchanga material is treated as the preferred source; computed values are never presented as official.

The current release covers 19 March 2026 through 6 April 2027. Dates with direct annual reference data are labelled validated. Dates reconstructed from calculations or cross-reference remain clearly labelled until checked against the corresponding printed annual Panchanga.
