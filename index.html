About

The app follows the Karnataka/Mysuru Chandra-mana and Amavasyanta tradition and uses the 2026–27 Ontikoppal-derived annual calendar as its primary date source. It is not affiliated with Ontikoppal Panchanga Mandira. Where the public annual extraction does not provide a field, the app does not pretend that the value came from Ontikoppal.

For religious use, compare the displayed result with your printed/local Panchanga when a tithi or nakshatra boundary is close to your puja time. The architecture is ready for a future publisher-supplied annual data pack containing exact ending times, Yoga, Karana and sunrise.
